About us

A crew that treats your home like their own

Palmetto Services LLC of SC is a locally owned and operated home services company dedicated to helping homeowners improve, protect, and enjoy their living spaces. Based right here in South Carolina, we take pride in delivering high-quality workmanship with a level of care that’s hard to find today.

We specialize in a wide range of home improvement and property-enhancement services — from interior and exterior renovations to custom carpentry, repairs, and security camera installations. Whether you’re updating a kitchen or adding peace of mind with a modern surveillance system, our goal is simple: reliable service and results you’ll be proud of.

What sets us apart is our commitment to honesty, clear communication, and doing the job right the first time. We treat every home as if it were our own, and every project — big or small — gets the same attention to detail.
